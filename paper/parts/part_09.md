APG V Classiﬁcation
Classiﬁcation of angiosperms (APG V)
Phylogenetically organized by orders arranged in a phylogenetic sequence; families alphabetically listed in orders. Families
published since 2016 are included as bracketed synonyms if not accepted here.
*changed circumscription of a family or newly added families since APG IV (2016).
†newly added or recircumscribed orders since APG IV (2016).
‡new relationship of major clades (not aﬀecting circumscription of orders).
Families with single quotation marks, for example, ‘Dioscoreaceae,’ are noted not to be monophyletic in the circumscription used
here and await more deﬁnitive phylogenetic analyses before formal classiﬁcation is settled.
